# Joining

A word is a chain of morphemes: `es` + `o` + `lu` + `zu` is `esoluzu`, *I
am being loved*. This page lists what stands between two morphemes when
they join. Most of the time it is nothing. The rules below say when it is a
sound, and which.

Three sounds do the joining. A prefix that meets a vowel takes `x`. A
suffix that meets a vowel takes `l`. Two consonants that cannot stand
together take the last vowel of the first morpheme.

# Moods

A mood is a suffix of one vowel that colours a word. On an action, it says
the intent: a caress, a push, a pull. On a thing, it says its style: loved,
or dancing. The moods let a speaker be subtle without many words. This page
lists the five moods, how they join, and the roots that were made with
them.

## The five moods

| Mood | Gloss | It says | Kind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `i` | `CARE` | caring, gentle | adverb |
| `o` | `PUSH` | pushing, away from | adverb |
| `u` | `PULL` | pulling, towards | adverb |
| `a` | `LOVED` | loved, taken inside, to the heart, to the self | adjective |
| `y` | `DANCE` | dancing, moving with no direction, drawing | adjective |

The adverbs `i`, `o` and `u` say how an action is done, and the word is an
action. The adjectives `a` and `y` say what a thing is like, and the word
stays a noun. The [Roles](./roles.html) page settles this.

Each of the five vowels is also a prefix: a subject, or the order `y`. The
[Subjects](./subjects.html) page lists both uses.

The Verbs card names only three moods, `i`, `o` and `u`. The moods `a` and
`y` came later: the note *Loved* of January 2022 says that `a` was kept
free for a late coming beauty.

## Other ways to say how

The Verbs card also says how an action is done with roots used as
suffixes: `au`, *lovingly*, `qer`, *fiercely*, `do`, *intensely*, and
`gi`, *gently, with love*. They are not moods. A later page will list
them.

## Roots made with a mood

Many roots of Zulapa were made from a root and a mood. They are roots of
their own now, and some changed shape.

| Root | From | Means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mi` | `ma` + `i` | a gentle hand, to caress |
| `mu` | `ma` + `u` | to pull, exhausted |
| `my` | `ma` + `y` | a trace, to draw |
| `mado` | `ma` + `o` | to slap |
| `ga` | `gu` + `a` | spirit |
| `gu` | `ga` + `u` | darkness, empathy, life |
| `gi` | `gu` + `i` | permanence, kind |
| `go` | `gu` + `o` | intense |
| `gai` | `ga` + `i` | consciousness, rain, gentle |
| `gau` | `ga` + `u` | attention |
| `gui` | `gu` + `i` | life, to give birth |
| `za` | `zu` + `a` | sap, to flow |
| `agi` | `aga` + `i` | story, epic |
| `ago` | `aga` + `o` | rhythm |
| `agu` | `aga` + `u` | ear, to listen |
| `odo` | `oda` + `o` | to leave |
| `odu` | `oda` + `u` | to come |
| `dao` | `da` + `o` | a distant place, from |
| `dau` | `da` + `u` | a close place, to |
| `liu` | `li` + `u` | to eat |
| `sheu` | `she` + `u` | nose, to breathe |
| `sheo` | `she` + `o` | to blow |
| `rumo` | `rum` + `o` | to throw |
| `lamu` | `lam` + `u` | to drink |

Some lost the vowel of the root, as `mi`, `ga` or `agu`. Some kept both
vowels, as `gai`, `liu` or `dau`. `mado` took a `d`.

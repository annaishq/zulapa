# Time

Zulapa says when an action happens on the action itself, and says it
finely: the same day, the day before, near, far, or maybe. This page lists
the tenses, the words of time, and the names of the days.

## The past is lived, the future is desired

The two tenses come from two roots. `em` is *life*, *to live*: the past is
what was lived. `ir` is *desire*, *to desire*: the future is what is
desired.

The same two roots make the words of time. `dem`, *when*, is `da` and
`em`: the place lived.

The past and the future can also be felt in the present. `lue` is `lu` and
`em`: the action happened in the past, and the body feels it now, as if it
were happening. `lui` is `lu` and `ir`: the action is desired so much that
it is felt real, and now. English has no word for either.

    o-lue-la                ti-la-m
    1-CONT.REMEMBER-kiss    2SG.POSS-lips-ACC
    'My lips are feeling your past kiss.'

## The tenses

A tense is a suffix on the action. A vowel after `em` or `ir` says how far
the action is from now.

| Vowel | How far | Past | Gloss | Future | Gloss |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | not said | `em` | `PST` | `ir` | `FUT` |
| `o` | the same day or night | `emo` | `PST.HOD` | `iro` | `FUT.HOD` |
| `i` | one day away | `emi` | `HEST` | `iri` | `CRAS` |
| `a` | near | `ema` | `PST.PROX` | `ira` | `FUT.PROX` |
| `u` | far | `emu` | `PST.DIST` | `iru` | `FUT.DIST` |
| `e` | maybe | `eme` | `PST.EVNT` | `ire` | `FUT.EVNT` |

The first two vowels are those of the subjects. `o` is *me*, the closest
one: `iro` is the closest future, later today or tonight. `i` is *you*, the
closest after me: `iri` is the next day.

Three more suffixes stand in the same place.

| Suffix | Gloss | It says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `lau` | `ETERN` | always |
| `nei` | `NEVER` | never |
| `lei` | `NOW` | now. With a subject that is not *you*, an order: *let us* |

The present has no mark.

## The words of time

| Word | It says |
| --- | --- |
| `dem` | when, at any time, in a phrase or in a question |
| `ju` | before |
| `xu` | after |
| `lu` | while |
| `uya` | since |
| `lulei` | until |
| `lau` | always |

## The days

`ya` is the night, and `yo` is the daytime.

| | Night | Daytime |
| --- | --- | --- |
| this one | `teya`, tonight | `teyo`, today |
| the one before | `emiya` | `emiyo`, yesterday |
| the one after | `iriya` | `iriyo`, tomorrow |
| some | `peya` | `peyo`, someday |
